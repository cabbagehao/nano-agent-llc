Frequently asked questions

What should I include in a support request?

Include the product, your account email when relevant, steps to reproduce the issue, expected and actual results, and device or browser details.

Can you help with a billing issue?

Yes. Choose “Billing or payment” and include the account email plus an order, invoice, or transaction reference. Do not include your full payment-card number.

How do I request account or data deletion?

Email us from the address associated with the account and name the product involved. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
