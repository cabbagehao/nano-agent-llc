About NanoAgent LLC

Pioneering Multi-Modal Artificial Intelligence SaaS for Creators & Global Software Developers

1. Our Mission

NanoAgent LLC (registered domain: nanoagent.app) was founded with a singular vision: to democratize state-of-the-art multi-modal Artificial Intelligence capabilities. We build ultra-fast, high-efficiency SaaS engines and APIs for Image synthesis, Video creation, and Audio processing.

2. Core Technological Focus

🖼️ NanoVision AI

Photorealistic diffusion engines, 8K Neural Upscaling, real-time background segmenters, and style transfer.

🎬 NanoFrame AI

Generative text-to-video rendering, temporal frame interpolation, 60fps upscale, and digital avatar lip-sync.

🎙️ NanoVoice AI

Zero-shot voice cloning, multilingual TTS synthesis, spatial audio separation, and AI noise suppression.

3. Commitment to the Apple Developer Ecosystem

As part of our commitment to high-performance computing, NanoAgent LLC designs software natively optimized for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 & A-series chips), using Apple CoreML and Metal Performance Shaders (MPS). This allows developers integrating our Swift SDK to deliver zero-latency AI capabilities on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro with industry-leading battery efficiency and on-device privacy protection.

4. Corporate Integrity & Compliance

NanoAgent LLC operates with strict corporate governance, maintaining full compliance with global legal frameworks including GDPR, CCPA, and Apple App Store Developer Review Guidelines. We prioritize user privacy, data security, and transparent operational practices.
